In this project, students used GIFs to explain complex internet algorithms and make them more accessible. Working in groups, they researched popular algorithms across different applications and made 4-8 GIFs explaining concepts around cryptocurrency or NFTs/digital collectibles, in regional languages for an Indian audience. It was set up as a challenge with specific requirements and constraints, a way to test how far GIFs could carry information to a wider audience.

**Key reference:** <span class="pub-title">The Visual Display of Quantitative Information</span>. E. R. Tufte. Graphics Press, 1983.
