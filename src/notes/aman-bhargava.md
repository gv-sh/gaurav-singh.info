Aman Bhargava is a designer and developer who works in data visualization, with a background in web development. I met him in 2021 at the [Mathematics of Magnificent Flora workshop](/notes/mathematics-of-magnificent-flora/) at Srishti Manipal Institute, where he was doing a Bachelor of Design in Human-Computer Interaction. His take on where design and technology meet was clear from the start.

Since then he has worked as a freelance developer at the Centre for Studies in Gender and Sexuality at Ashoka University, as a graphics intern at Reuters, and as a Data and Design Fellow at Civic Data Lab, where he got better at turning complex data into stories people can read. He cares about the social and cultural side of data as much as the technical one, and he thinks a good visualisation can be a tool for education and change. He now works full-time at Revival Labs.
