Co-facilitated with Riyaz Shaikh. This course was for students who already had a basic grounding in interaction design and human-computer interaction. It pushed them to think critically about where computing and technology are heading, and how to design for that. We kept putting hard questions to them to get them thinking past the conventional methods. The course ran as collaborative studio work, with students building up their design skills together.

**Key reference:** <span class="pub-title">Interaction Design: Beyond Human-Computer Interaction</span>. Y. Rogers, H. Sharp, J. Preece. Wiley, 2023.
