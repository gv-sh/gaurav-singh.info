I first met Prakhar Ojha at Srishti in 2013, when we were both doing the Advanced Diploma in Innovation, Experience and Design. It was clear early on that he could hold imaginative thinking and practical problem-solving at the same time.

His fascination with stories, in movies and games, shapes how he designs. He's good at reading what users actually need underneath what they say, a sense sharpened by his design studies and by his own life.

He has since worked as a Product Designer at Moonraft and UST, improving user experiences and tightening design processes. As Lead in Experience Design at Moonraft, working with Continental, he built a digital material library. At Olam Group he was central to developing the Adva app, aimed at more sustainable living.

These days Prakhar works in experience and interaction design, pulling narrative together with user-centred design to make digital products that are both useful and engaging.
