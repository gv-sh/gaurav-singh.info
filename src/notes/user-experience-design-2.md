Co-facilitated with Dr. Riyaz Shaikh. This was an introductory course on user experience. Students looked at existing and upcoming products and technologies, picked apart the parts of the experience that had been deliberately designed, and speculated on where they might go. They used Wizard of Oz and other techniques to prototype and get feedback from their peers.

**Key reference:** <span class="pub-title">The Elements of User Experience: User-Centered Design for the Web</span>. J. J. Garrett. New Riders, 2002.
