Karan Dudeja is a digital product designer. He builds interfaces that let creative people move through digital tools with the same freedom they'd have on a canvas, and you can see the thinking behind the designs as much as the craft in them. We met at the Srishti-Manipal Institute between 2018 and 2021, where his work was varied and always aimed at engagement.

He is now at Stockholm University, looking at how immersive technology might change the way we work with the digital. His grounding in human-computer interaction shows in how he treats virtual and augmented reality: not as ends in themselves but as ways to extend what people can make. He is adaptable, collaborative, and a genuine listener, and that openness tends to lift the people around him.
