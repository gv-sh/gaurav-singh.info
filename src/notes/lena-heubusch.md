Lena Heubusch is an artist and designer whose work explores the connections between the material, the intuitive, and the sensitive, with a research focus she calls "being body."

I worked with Lena on "Together with Touch," a real highlight of 2022 for me. We were introduced through mutual contacts, and from the start she took the time to understand my work and how I approach things, which made the whole project feel collaborative. She led with clarity and vision while staying realistic about our goals and timelines, a balance I respect, and she made sure everyone felt credited for what they put in. It's rare to find someone that good at their own practice who also lifts the people around them. We've stayed in touch since, and those exchanges have been their own reward. I'm hoping we get to work together again.
