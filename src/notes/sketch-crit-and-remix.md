This workshop pushed at how we understand the current roles of digital and technology. Rather than staying inside their own inquiries, participants stepped back to ask how you carry out an inquiry about digital and technology in the first place. It offered examples, methods, and frameworks for imagining and building futures around computation and technology. The format was challenge-based, with a daily challenge and about 7-8 hours of work a day. There were group activities through the week, and participants left with ways to take their own inquiries forward.
